selected projects
Deep FOCUS — SMLM Super-Resolution
2025 MSc dissertation
Focal Loss U-Net PyTorch Microscopy CUDA

Systematic comparative analysis of loss functions for single-molecule localisation microscopy. Generated a 10,000-sample synthetic dataset via ThunderSTORM, trained modified U-Net models (1→32→64→128→512ch, 8× upsampling), and demonstrated that Focal Loss achieves PSNR 33.8 dB — 60–100% over MSE and L1 baselines. Implications for sparse detection in medical imaging and astronomy.
